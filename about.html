I started in computer engineering, not marketing. That background shapes everything about how I work now — not as a talking point, but as the actual method.

Marketing has a lot of moving, connected parts: ad platforms, CRMs, content tools, analytics, automation layers. Most marketers treat these as separate tasks. I treat them as a system — one where a change in one part predictably affects the others, and where problems get diagnosed, not guessed at.

A debugging mindset means I don't accept "the campaign underperformed" as an answer — I trace it back to the specific input, whether that's a tracking gap, a broken audience signal, or a content bottleneck. Systems thinking means I design processes to be repeatable, not one-off. And practical API and automation skills mean that when a workflow can be automated, I build it instead of doing it manually every time.

I'm currently building out real client work across all five service areas — you can follow along on the Portfolio page.

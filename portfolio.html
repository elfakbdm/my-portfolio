E-Commerce Funnel Analysis & A/B Test Design

Problem

Needed to find and quantify the biggest conversion bottleneck across 99K+ real e-commerce orders (Olist dataset).

Approach

Reconstructed the full purchase funnel with SQL, traced the largest drop-off (Order Approved → Delivered, 1,724 orders lost) to stock and fulfillment issues, then designed a statistically rigorous A/B test (α = 0.05, 80% power) to validate a fix.

Result

Calculated a required sample size of 8,842 orders to detect a 1-point improvement in delivery rate — turning an analysis into a testable business hypothesis.
